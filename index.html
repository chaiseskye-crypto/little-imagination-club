MADE FOR IMAGINATION

From a drawing to a storybook

The goal is to keep the child's own artwork at the heart of every story, with tools that help families turn their ideas into a book.

🎨

1. Add a drawing

Choose a picture and tell us a little about the characters, places or adventure behind it.

✨

2. Create a story

Choose a theme, reading level and ending, then shape the words around the child's imagination.

📚

3. Make a book

Review the pages, edit the story and print or save a copy when the book-making features are connected.
